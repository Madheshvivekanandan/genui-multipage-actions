## Part 1 · The RFP

### Summary
Pinecrest Outdoor Co. seeks an agency for a brand refresh and a spring 2027
digital marketing campaign. The work combines brand research, identity
refresh, campaign development, media buying, digital execution, in-store
materials and measurement. The incumbent agency is participating in the review.
Proposals are due November 20, 2026, 5:00 PM CST. The engagement covers fiscal 2027.

### Who they are
- A regional outdoor retailer, based in Wisconsin.
- Sells camping, hiking and fishing gear.
- 14 stores across the Upper Midwest, plus an e-commerce business growing against rising competition.
- Contact: Marketing Department, [PRIVATE_EMAIL].
- The brief is brand-led and uses weighted evaluation criteria.

### Their stated goal
> "Refresh the Pinecrest brand identity (logo evolution, color palette, voice
> and tone) to appeal to younger outdoor enthusiasts without alienating our
> loyal family base."

Stated outcomes:
- Increase e-commerce revenue by 30% year over year.
- Grow loyalty program membership from 85,000 to 120,000 members by end of 2027.
- Improve brand awareness among 22–35 year olds in Milwaukee, Madison, Minneapolis and Chicago.
- Launch an integrated spring 2027 campaign across paid social, search, CTV and in-store.

### What they're asking for
1. Brand strategy and positioning research, including customer interviews and competitive audit.
2. Visual identity refresh and brand guidelines.
3. Campaign creative concept, with at least three initial directions.
4. Media planning and buying across paid social, paid search, connected TV, and out-of-home.
5. Website landing pages and email nurture sequences.
6. In-store signage and point-of-sale materials for 14 locations.
7. Measurement framework and monthly reporting dashboard.

Key dates:
- November 20, 2026, 5:00 PM CST: proposal due.
- December 15, 2026: agency selection.
- February 1, 2027: brand research complete.
- March 1, 2027: brand refresh approved.

### Requirements
- R1 · Disqualifier · Submit the proposal by November 20, 2026, 5:00 PM CST.
- R2 · Process gate · Agency overview and relevant retail / outdoor category experience.
- R3 · Disqualifier · Two case studies with measurable results.
- R4 · Process gate · Proposed team and roles.
- R5 · Process gate · Approach and methodology.
- R6 · Disqualifier · Detailed fee breakdown.
- R7 · Disqualifier · Three client references.
- R8 · Disqualifier · Agency fees should not exceed $300,000.

### Money and decision
Total budget: $750,000 for fiscal 2027, of which approximately $450,000 is
working media. Agency fees should not exceed $300,000.

How they will evaluate:
- Strategic thinking and understanding of our audience: 30%
- Creative quality: 25%
- Relevant experience: 20%
- Cost and value: 15%
- Team chemistry and fit: 10%

The RFP names the Marketing Department as contact. It does not name the
evaluators or the final decision-maker.
